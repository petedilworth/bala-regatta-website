Documented by the official programme, sponsored by the Bala Aquatic Association.

The programme calls this the "99.5 Annual" regatta — the count was held back a year so that
2009, the centenary of the 1909 founding, could be the 100th. No edition number is recorded
here for that reason; by the pre-2008 count it would have been the 100th.
