name: "Hugh Carmichael Spirit of the Regatta Award"
aliases:
  - "Spirit of the Regatta Award"
  - "Hugh Carmichael - Spirit of the Regatta"
event: spirit-of-the-regatta
---

Named in honour of Hugh Carmichael and given to people or families who have made outstanding contributions to the regatta and embodied its spirit over many years. Not awarded every year; the 2026 programme lists every recipient since 1989.

Winners are recorded as each programme printed them; spellings of the same name vary
between programmes and are kept as printed.
